FAQs

Get Every Single Answer From Here

1. What makes Dosa Dosa one of the best South Indian restaurants in Juhu?

Dosa Dosa offers authentic South Indian flavours inspired by Chennai's culinary heritage. From freshly prepared dosas and idlis to traditional filter coffee and regional specialities, every dish is crafted using premium ingredients and time-tested recipes.

2. Do you offer Jain-friendly South Indian food?

Yes. We offer a variety of Jain-friendly South Indian dishes, making Dosa Dosa a preferred dining destination for guests with specific dietary preferences while maintaining authentic flavours.

3. What are the must-try dishes at Dosa Dosa?

Some of our customer favourites include Benne Podi Masala Dosa, Ghee Paper Dosa, Pesarattu with Andhra Peanut Chutney, Set Dosa & Vada Curry, Kanchipuram Idli, and our authentic South Indian Filter Coffee.

4. Do you serve healthy dosa and idli options?

Absolutely. Our menu includes nutritious choices such as Ragi Dosa, Moong Dal Dosa, Multigrain Dosa, Ragi Idli, Bajra Idli, Jowar Idli, and other wholesome South Indian specialities prepared fresh daily.
