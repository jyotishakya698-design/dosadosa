Our Story

Bringing Chennai's Authentic Flavours To Mumbai

Dosa Dosa was born from a passion for preserving authentic South Indian culinary traditions while creating a modern dining experience for Mumbai's food lovers. Our goal is simple: serve the finest dosas and South Indian specialities using time-tested recipes, premium ingredients, and traditional preparation techniques.

Every dosa batter is carefully fermented, every chutney is freshly prepared, and every cup of filter coffee is brewed to perfection. Today, Dosa Dosa is recognised as one of the best South Indian restaurants in Juhu and a favourite destination for breakfast lovers across Mumbai.

Located near Juhu Beach, we bring together healthy recipes, high-quality ingredients, and modern dining to create an unforgettable South Indian food experience. From our signature Benne Dosas and Ghee Roasts to wholesome Millet Dosas and handcrafted beverages, every dish is prepared fresh and served with our signature chutneys and sambar.

As a pure vegetarian restaurant, we also offer a wide range of Jain-friendly options, making Dosa Dosa the perfect destination for families, food lovers, and guests with specific dietary preferences. Our commitment to authentic flavours ensures that everyone can enjoy a delicious South Indian meal without compromising.

Whether you're looking for the best dosa in Mumbai, a healthy South Indian breakfast, a pure vegetarian family restaurant in Juhu, or a restaurant serving Jain South Indian food near Juhu Beach, Dosa Dosa is your destination.
